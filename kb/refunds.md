# Refund policy

Tasklane offers a full refund within 30 days of the first payment on any paid plan. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 30 days of renewal. After 30 days, refunds are not automatic and must be approved by the billing team.

To request a refund, go to Settings > Billing > Request refund, or reply to your receipt email. Approved refunds reach the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days.
