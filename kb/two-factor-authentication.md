# Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Turn on 2FA under Profile > Security. Tasklane supports authenticator apps and gives you 10 single-use recovery codes when you enable it.

If you lose your phone, sign in with a recovery code and re-enroll a new device. If you have no recovery codes, support must verify your identity before 2FA can be reset; this is handled by a human agent and takes 1 to 2 business days.
